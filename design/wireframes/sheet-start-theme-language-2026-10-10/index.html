Тема 1 · Аркуш вибору з «Почати» · телефон 375×812

Signed structure (SD defaults)

  • Sheet order (DOM = visual): size radiogroup «Розмір поля» → level radiogroup «Складність» (reason line first when 4×4 is marked) → «Почати» → «Закрити». Group names stay aria-labels, not visible headings (as today).
  • A press on a size or level only marks it (ring ◉ and border; the sheet stays open, focus stays on the pressed option). The summary keeps showing the board shown: «6×6 · Розминка ▾».
  • One sticky footer at the bottom of the sheet: «Почати» primary (dark fill, ⅔ width, bold), «Закрити» secondary (outline, ⅓). Both 44 px tall. Both stay direct children of the sheet (no wrapper): the sheet is a grid, the other children span both columns, the two buttons sit in the last row with position: sticky; bottom: 0 and the existing mask shadow, so they stay visible when the sheet scrolls inside itself.
  • «Почати» left, «Закрити» right = DOM and Tab order, and the same action-then-cancel order as the confirm dialog («Так, почати» | «Скасувати»).

Footer sub-choice (the one you may pick)

  • Footer-1, side by side (recommended): adds 0 px, because «Закрити» already takes that row. The thumb reaches the wide «Почати» on the left; «Закрити» stays easy to hit (≈ 110 px × 44 at 375, ≈ 93 px at 320).
  • Footer-2, stacked: +52 px (44 + 8 gap). Fine at 375×812, but it brings back inner scroll on short desktops (see the desktop sheet) and makes 320×700 scroll more.

Height budget at 375×812

  • Bottom sheet cap 88dvh = 714 px. Today's 6×6 sheet is 535 px (277–812), 4×4 570 px.
  • Footer-1: 6×6 ≈ 535, 4×4 ≈ 570 → fits, no inner scroll. Footer-2: ≈ 587 / 622 → still fits.
  • The play screen itself is unchanged (NFR-10 not touched).
  • 320×700 (not drawn): cap 556 px; the 6×6 sheet already scrolls a little today; with Footer-1 it is unchanged, with Footer-2 +52 px more to scroll. The footer stays visible either way (sticky).

Requirement ids

  • FR-96 (children in order, «Почати» before «Закрити»), FR-100 (marked choice), FR-101 («Почати» 44 px), FR-95 (summary = board shown), FR-91 (4×4 by marked size), FR-97 (Закрити discards), NFR-12 (44 px). No new id (A-41 stays 4).
