Бінарка: sound prototype

Design tool, not the game. Nothing plays until you click. Use headphones and, if you can, a phone speaker too. Recipes and the cue rule: design/audio/sound-design.md.

Level and the proposed toggle

Default 70 is the design's master gain. Turning «Звук» on plays soundOn; turning it off cuts any sound within 12 ms.

Cues

The dashed button is a candidate the design keeps silent, here only so you can decide.

Stacking (fast input)

Each new input cue cuts the previous one (12 ms fade), so taps should stay separate taps and never pile up into a buzz.

Scene

place0, place1, place0, conflict, place1, clear, place1, hint, place0, win, with gaps like a calm player.

Play in context

The real engine with the proposed cue rule: one action, at most one cue; win > conflict > the action's own cue. Seeds 1, 2, 3, … in order.
