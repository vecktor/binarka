# Design: update-settings-panel

## Context

G2 wave 2, the settings block, implemented by its own session in its own worktree. The signed text is A-61, FR-65, FR-117, A-57 and NFR-14 in `docs/requirements.md` and `docs/handoff/settings-panel-amendment-draft-2026-10-12.md` (OQ-1 A, SP-1 A, defaults for OQ-2 to OQ-6; autonomy-log row 193). The confirmation dialog has its own folder (`update-confirm-dialog`) and its own session.

Today (`src/ui/style.css`): `.settings` is a bottom sheet (`max-height: 55dvh`, padding `24px 16px 16px`, no rule at 22.5rem and narrower) and, from 48rem, the dropdown under the header; fill, border and shadows come from the shell. `.theme-control` and `.language-control` are the design's card; their options are `--color-text` at 16px and weight 400, `flex: 1 1 auto`; the chosen option is `--color-given-bg` with a 2px `--color-focus` frame and an inset 1px `--color-focus` ring. `.settings-close` has the base `button` padding (48px tall), is not sticky, and has weight 700. The size options in the setup sheet already have the design's segmented paint (`update-setup-sheet`). The design (`design/v0/app/binarka.css`, `review-set-14`) paints the three segmented controls with one rule and gives the three panels one phone rule.

## Goals

- The settings panel has the design's size on the phone (the 22.5rem padding, «Закрити» at 44px), the segmented paint on the theme and language options, and the design's sticky «Закрити», in light and dark.
- The 10 phone settings shots of run 12 rise; no other shot of the gate falls.
- A focused option on a short window is never hidden under the sticky «Закрити».
- The stylesheet stays inside the colour scan, and the shell's tests and block B's tests of `.size-control` stay green unedited, except the two deliberate replacements.

## Non-goals

- Hover, press and the panel's slide (OQ-5); a new colour token or contrast pair (OQ-1 A); the dropdown from 48rem (not edited); the confirmation dialog; any change to «Borders, cues and focus rings have enough contrast» or to a token or pair count; a 0.98 promise per shot; Safari and Firefox.
- No edit to `design/v0`, to the locked adapter `scripts/check-visual-parity-adapters.mjs`, to `playwright.config.ts`, to `src/ui/play-page.ts`, or to a requirement other than the five named in `proposal.md`.

## Decisions

1. **Three rules, not one selector list (OQ-3).** The design writes `.size-control, .theme-control, .language-control` once. The page keeps one rule per control and a stylesheet test keeps the shared declarations equal. Trade-off: some repetition in the stylesheet; in return block B's tests of `.size-control` (which read `.size-control button` by its exact selector) stay as they are, and a change to one rule cannot silently drift because the equality test names the declaration. The alternative (the design's list) would touch another block's archived tests for no pixel gain.
2. **The same literal forms as `.size-control button`.** The test compares declaration values as written, so the options say `padding: 0 0.5rem`, `border-radius: calc(var(--radius-m) - 0.1875rem)`, `font-size: 0.9375rem`, `border-width: 2px` and so on, character for character like the size rule, not an equivalent (`8px`, `7px`). Trade-off: a stricter test, one more reason a future tidy-up fails loudly. The cards `.theme-control` and `.language-control` (3px padding and gap, 10px radius) already equal the design's values and are not edited.
3. **The option fill is `--color-control-bg`, not `transparent`.** The design's option is transparent over the card's `--surface`; «The theme options set their own colours» asks each option rule for a `var(--color-...)` fill, so the page states the card's own token. The computed paint test therefore compares an unchosen option's `background-color` with the design card's, not with the design option's transparent fill.
4. **The cue is the weight and the appearing frame (OQ-2 default).** The checked rule loses the inner `box-shadow` ring; WCAG 1.4.1 is met by the weight (700 against 500) and the frame (a transparent 2px border becomes `--color-primary`), the design's own cues. The scenarios that asked for a `border-style`, `border-width`, `box-shadow` or `text-decoration` change deliberately and now ask for `font-weight` (at least 700 against at most 500) and a `border-color` that differs from a transparent one. Alternative B (keep the inner ring as an extra cue) was declined: a small pixel mismatch in every settings shot.
5. **The ring over a chosen option's frame is an accepted adjacency (OQ-1 A); no pair is added.** The ring (3px, `outline-offset: -1px`) covers the outer pixel of the 2px frame, so its inner side touches `--color-primary` at 2.94 light and 1.88 dark, while its outer band lies on the card fill at 15.88 / 13.85. This is the same pair as the cell ring against a hinted neighbour's primary border, which PA-Q8 and A-57 already accept. It covers the size options too (block B gave them the same frame; until now an unrecorded failure; the `-1px` offset of the size options is the page's own, the design keeps 2px there). **No pair is added to `CONTRAST_PAIRS`** because `--color-cell-bg` has the same value as `--color-control-bg` in both sets and is already paired with `--color-focus` (15.88 / 13.85, the group `focus-ring`); a second pair would state the same number twice. The scenario «The ring's accepted adjacencies are recorded» gains the bound (at least 13.85), the equality of the two fills and the absence of the pair, so a change that moves the adjacency is deliberate. Alternatives: B (a page-only frame token `--color-option-checked`, a recorded deviation from the design and a token for both wave-2 blocks) and C (keep the dark frame for theme and language) were declined by the user. Not ADR-worthy by itself: A-61 records it as A-57 records the cell ring's three.
6. **«Закрити» is sticky with the mask, and the open panel reserves it in `scroll-padding-bottom` (OQ-6 A).** The design's shared `.setup-close, .settings-close` rule is `position: sticky; bottom: 0` with a mask of two spread shadows in the raised fill; the page's `.rules-close` already has the same two shadows, which the new rule copies. Without a scroll padding the browser's own focus scrolling brings a focused option to the scrollport's edge, under the sticky button (FR-65 "unobscured", WCAG 2.4.11). `.settings:popover-open` therefore declares `scroll-padding-bottom` of at least `calc(2.75rem + 2rem + 2px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`, as `.setup-sheet:popover-open` does. The arithmetic (folder audit, finding 2): Chromium keeps a stuck sticky box inside the scroller's padding, so the stuck «Закрити» ends 16px above the scrollport's bottom (the design's comment that the second shadow "covers the panel's bottom padding", `binarka.css:541-542`; the setup sheet's `+24px` that "missed the 16px bottom padding", `docs/qa/update-setup-sheet-start/focus-obscured-repro.txt`); the mask's top is then 16 + 44 + 16 = 76px above it, and a focused option's ring reaches 2px below the option's box, so the padding needs at least 78px plus the safe-area inset. The short-window walk decides the fit; A-61's "at least the height of «Закрити» plus its mask" is met by the larger value. The panel scrolls only when the window is short (the sheet under about 505px tall, the dropdown under about 383px), so no gate shot shows it; a short-window Tab walk (667×375, 568×320, 1024×360) is the proof, and it asserts its premise (the panel scrolls) so that a window where it does not scroll fails instead of passing vacuously. Trade-off: one more rule and one more e2e; alternative B (keep the button static, no mask) costs no pixels and no obscuring risk but departs from the design's behaviour. The setup sheet's «Закрити» stays unmasked (its footer strip masks, A-60).
7. **`.settings-close` stays one top-level rule; the 22.5rem padding is a later block.** The shell's test (`tests/play-page-panel-shell-stylesheet.test.ts` 25, 139) reads exactly one top-level `.settings` rule and exactly one top-level `.settings-close` rule, finds the first block of the selector in the file as the top-level one, and counts the shell's three declarations (`background-color`, `border-color`, `box-shadow`) once in it; it also counts the shadow rules of `.rules`, `.setup-sheet`, `.settings` and `.confirm` (7). So: the sticky, the mask, the padding, the border and the weight go into the one `.settings-close` rule (the mask is a `box-shadow` on `.settings-close`, which is not among the counted selectors); the phone `max-height` pair and `padding` change in the top-level `.settings` rule without touching the shell's three; `padding-top: 1rem` goes in `@media (max-width: 22.5rem)` in a block that comes after the top-level rule; the scroll padding goes in `.settings:popover-open`, which declares no `display`. The 48rem rule is not edited. If a shell test still fails, it is a finding for the user, not a test to edit.
8. **The theme options grow by content, the language options are equal halves (OQ-4).** `flex: 1 1 auto` keeps «Як у системі» from truncating (the design's own exception); `flex: 1 1 0` gives «Українська» and "English" equal halves, as the design. The stylesheet test pins both values; the e2e measures the halves (within 0.5 px).
9. **The A-61 values the draft's test list omits are pinned too.** A-61 states `max-height` `60vh` then `55dvh`, the safe-area padding and the `--color-control-border` 2px border of «Закрити»; section 5 of the draft lists the tests only in part. They are in the ADDED requirement (scenarios «The phone regime…» and «…sticky with the mask») because A-61 says them and the 4px and 12px of the failing shots come from them. See open point 1.
10. **The geometry cases go under their own key, `settingsPanel`, and carry the computed paint.** `e2e/nfr-14-layout-geometry.spec.ts` pins `fixture.cases` to the 32 layout shots, and the keys `rulesPanel` and `setupSheet` exist (and `confirmDialog` after the dialog block). The key holds `selectors` (`[data-section="settings"]`, `[data-control="theme"]`, `[data-control="language"]`, `[data-control="theme"] button[aria-checked="true"]`, `[data-control="language"] button[aria-checked="true"]`, `[data-action="settings-close"]`; the design's DOM carries the same hooks, `design/v0/components/binarka-page.tsx` 431-471), `coverage: "sampled"`, its own provenance and 7 cases: `<w>-light-settings` at 320, 375, 768, 1024, 1366 and 1440 (the design's `/settings/` route, open at load; the 6×6 fixture; «Як у системі» chosen) and `375-light-settings-dark` (the design's `/settings-dark/` route, «Темна» chosen, so a chosen option other than the first is measured). The page side replays a recorded `afterLoad: "open-settings"` (press the settings button, wait for `:popover-open`; the design routes are open at load, a press would close them) and, for the dark case, a recorded `storedTheme: "dark"`: the page's stored preference `localStorage['binarka.theme']` is seeded before the load through an init script, as the capture adapter does for `settings-dark` shots (`scripts/check-visual-parity-adapters.mjs` ~305-321), and the design side needs no step because its route is already dark. Each case records the design's `boxes` of the six elements, and the design's computed `color`, `background-color`, `border-top-color`, `border-top-width`, `font-weight` and `font-size` of one unchosen and one chosen option of each control, so the paint scenario needs no second fixture and `quality/design-paint.json` (the main column's) is not touched. No deviation is recorded: unlike the setup sheet the panel's box is the design's (the page's cap `max-width: calc(100% - 2rem)` is not reached at any sampled window). The freeze adds the key and changes no existing case, selector or provenance field (the provenance guard of `scripts/freeze-design-geometry.mjs`).
11. **The dialog block merges first; this block rebases onto it before the refreeze (SP-1 A).** See "The split".

## Error handling strategy

- The change is stylesheet-only; there is no new runtime error path. A wrong token, a stray old binding, a media block out of order or a second top-level rule is a stylesheet test failure that names the rule and the declaration.
- The e2e fails (never skips) with the case, the element and both boxes on any difference; it asserts the panel is `:popover-open` before it reads, and fails with the freeze instruction if the key `settingsPanel` is absent. The freeze script fails with the build instruction of `design/README.md` when `design/v0/out/index.html` is missing.
- The focus walk asserts its premise at each window; a window where the panel does not scroll fails the premise and is a finding for the user, not a reason to drop the window.
- A capture-unstable run is a harness defect to file, not a pixel finding (AGENTS.md, capture determinism).

## Pixel expectations

- **The 10 phone settings shots fail in run 12** (table in `proposal.md`); the 16 from 768 pass but several sit just over the line.
- **Expected, not measured: the 10 phone shots rise.** The sheet's box and the chosen options' paint are the measured causes; `node scripts/visual-block.mjs --block settings` localises any residual. No 0.98 claim per shot. **If a settings shot does not rise, the block stops and explains it before the review gate.** A fall is reported as measured, never argued away.
- The focus shots (`*-settings-focus`) focus «Темна» while «Як у системі» is chosen, so no gate shot shows a focused chosen option and the accepted adjacency costs no pixel.

## The split

Repeated from section 9 of the settings draft; the same text is in the `design.md` of `update-confirm-dialog`.

- **What the two blocks share.** No token (the dialog declares `--color-violation-ink`, this block none). No geometry or paint rule of `src/ui/style.css`: this block edits `.settings`, `.settings:popover-open`, `.theme-control`, `.language-control` and `.settings-close`; the dialog block edits `.confirm` and `.confirm-buttons`; the `:root` blocks change only by the dialog's token. Both add cases to `quality/design-geometry.json` (keys `settingsPanel` and `confirmDialog`) and a block to `scripts/visual-block.mjs` (`settings`, `confirm`): a mechanical conflict for whichever merges second.
- **SP-1 A: no shared slice.** Nothing is used by both blocks. The two sessions start at once from `main`. **The dialog block merges first** (it is smaller and owns the token pins); this block rebases onto it, re-runs its battery and a fresh `check:visual`, and merges. If this block is ready first it may merge first on the user's word, and the dialog block rebases.
- **Requirements, each MODIFIED by exactly one folder.** This block owns: «Cells and buttons show a visible, unobscured focus indicator», «Settings button and panel», «The theme options set their own colours», «The setup sheet takes the design's size, place and paint» (its guard sentence at ~l. 4647 and the scenario clause at ~l. 4718, nothing else of it), and the ADDED «The settings panel takes the design's size, place and paint». It does NOT touch «Borders, cues and focus rings have enough contrast», any token or pair count, or «Confirmation before discarding player entries». The dialog block owns «Borders…» (its token table, pair list, counts, and the role column, where it words the roles of `--color-text-muted`, `--color-primary` and `--color-primary-soft` in the theme and language options from A-61) and the count-only deltas of «Main column layout geometry follows the design reference», «The panels take the shared surface paint» and «The rules panel takes the design's size, place and paint». «The main column takes the design palette», «The backdrops take the design's translucent tokens» and «Segmented options keep the focus ring inside the card» are modified by nobody.
- **Design freezes.** `quality/design-geometry.json` is refrozen by one stream at a time: the dialog block adds `confirmDialog` first; **this block adds `settingsPanel` after rebasing onto the dialog's merge** (or the user decides the order). The design does not change, so a refreeze adds cases and must change no existing case.
- **Shared files and the expected rebase conflicts.** `src/ui/style.css`: each block only its own rules (perhaps neighbouring lines). Three test files are edited by both blocks at different lines, textual conflicts only: `tests/play-page-theme-stylesheet.test.ts` (the dialog's token pin; this block's cue tests at 228-288), `tests/play-page-setup-sheet-stylesheet.test.ts` (the dialog's pair pin; this block's guard at 598-602), `tests/play-page-palette-stylesheet.test.ts` (the dialog's title; this block's adjacency test at 97). `quality/design-geometry.json` and `scripts/visual-block.mjs` conflict mechanically. `README.md` and the baseline spec after the first archive conflict too. `src/ui/play-page.ts`: neither block. The token and pair pins: the dialog block only.
- **Captures.** Each session sets its own `BINARKA_PORT_BASE` (6473 for the settings block and 6373 for the dialog block; `node scripts/capture-ports.mjs` prints the ports), so no lock is needed.
- **Not delivered here.** Hover, press and the slide; Safari and Firefox; the shell's historical not-delivered sentence about «Закрити» of the settings panel is read as history.

## Risks and mitigations

- **The short-window walk may fail** (a focused option hidden, or the panel not scrolling at a sampled window). The scroll padding is a lower bound; the walk decides. Mitigation: the walk is written with the port's rule and asserts its premise; a failure is a finding for the user (open point 3); nothing is loosened.
- **The shell's tests fail on the merged rules** (decision 7). Mitigation: merge declarations into the one top-level rule, re-declare none of the shell's three; report, never edit the shell's tests.
- **The rebase over the dialog block conflicts in the pin files** (the split). Mitigation: the dialog's pins are taken as they stand; the settings tests that read the lists compare them as lists against a copy refreshed at the rebase from `git show main:tests/helpers/css.ts` (never from this branch, so a token added by mistake is not copied in), not as counts; the test is a GUARD, and the real guards of the counts are the dialog's pins and task 4.4's no-`:root` filter.
- **A settings shot falls or does not rise.** The block stops and explains it.
- **The dark options on the card are `#24201b` on the raised `#2c2620`.** As the design's own `--surface` controls; not a defect.
- **A taller or shorter «Закрити» moves the phone sheet's lines** (below 48rem the panel grows upward from the bottom edge). That is the point of the change; the geometry cases compare every box to 0.5 px.

## Open points

1. **Values beyond the draft's test list.** A-61 states the `60vh` fallback, the safe-area padding and the `--color-control-border` border of «Закрити»; section 5 of the draft names the stylesheet tests only in part. They are pinned in the ADDED requirement. The user may strike the fallback and the safe-area term (no pixel cost; they are the design's own phone rule).
2. **Historical sentences left as they are.** «The panels take the shared surface paint» says «Закрити» of the settings panel is not sticky (what the shell change did); the setup-sheet requirement's not-delivered item (1) says the theme and language options' paint is the second wave. Both are read as history (the first by the draft, the second by the same reading) and not edited, so as not to touch another block's requirement text beyond the two signed sentences.
3. **The focus walk's premises** at 667×375, 568×320 and 1024×360 are asserted (the panel scrolls; «Закрити» is stuck at the panel's bottom, so the walk cannot pass because the button scrolled away); if the panel does not scroll at one of them, the user is asked (the window changes, not the premise).
4. **The scenario «The settings panel follows the column from 48rem»** (in «Main column layout geometry follows the design reference») keeps its name and text and must stay green; the new geometry scenario has its own name.
5. **The scenario rename** named in "What was checked": decided by the user on 2026-10-12 at about 12:33, «Rename as written» (the new heading stays true; the comments at `tests/play-page-setup-sheet-stylesheet.test.ts:584` and `:598` follow it; `docs/qa/update-settings-panel/folder-audit.md` note 9).
