# Design: update-page-palette

## Context

G2 block 2 (autonomy-log rows 148, 154, 156 and 157). The page and the design build share the markup and the `data-*` contract of the main column, so the design's paint ports rule by rule. What is new against block 1 is that paint is pinned by signed rules (FR-65, A-51, FR-64) and by tests, so the port is a negotiation between the design and those rules. The negotiation was done in `docs/handoff/palette-amendment-draft-2026-10-10.md` (SIGNED; conflict map C1 to C20) and folded into `docs/requirements.md` (FR-65, A-51, A-57 with the appendix). This design records how the port applies it. The values of the 25 tokens are in the spec delta and in the appendix to A-57; they are not repeated here.

## Goals

- The paint of the main column equals the design build's at the sampled cases, light and dark, except three recorded deviations.
- Every signed contrast pair holds for both token sets, by test, on the literal values.
- The stylesheet stays inside the colour scan: tokens only, no `color-mix(`, no `!important`, one `:has(`.

## Non-goals

- The sheet, panels, dialog and backdrops (later blocks); «Почати» (sheet block).
- Pixel floors per shot or per block; the hover filter and pressed feedback.
- Changing the design: a design round is the way to adopt the three deviations (PA-Q2 C, PA-Q3 C), and it is not part of this change.

## Decisions

1. **Token mapping: one `--color-*` token per design colour (A-57).** The design's `--bg`, `--ink`, `--ink-muted`, `--surface`, `--line`, `--cell-border`, `--given-bg`, `--given-ink`, `--entry-ink`, `--primary`, `--primary-ink`, `--primary-soft`, `--violation`, `--violation-bg`, `--violation-ink`, `--success`, `--success-bg` and `--focus` map to `--color-page`, `-text`, `-text-muted`, `-cell-bg` and `-control-bg` (both from `--surface`), `-line`, `-cell-border` and `-control-border` (both from `--cell-border`), `-given-bg`, `-given-text`, `-entry-text`, `-primary`, `-primary-text`, `-primary-soft`, `-violation-border`, `-violation-bg`, `-violation-text` (light from `--violation-ink`, dark from `--violation`), `-success`, `-success-bg` and `-focus`. The 13 baseline names keep their role. Where two names share a value (`--color-cell-bg` and `--color-control-bg`; `--color-cell-border` and `--color-given-border`), both are kept: a role is a name, and a later block may split a pair without renaming. Trade-off: more names than distinct colours; accepted, because renaming later breaks every test that binds a name.
2. **Only the 25 tokens of block 2 are declared.** The appendix signs 34; the 9 "later" tokens are declared by the block that binds them, so the stylesheet never holds an unused token. A scenario checks that each declared token is referenced. Trade-off: each later block needs a small spec delta; accepted (A-57: "a later block adds its rows from the appendix").
3. **Three deviations from the design, each needed by a signed pair (A-57).**
   - The light cell border and given border are `#847b6d`, not the design's `#8c8374`: the design's value is 2.71:1 against the board plate, which the page now has as `--color-line`; `#847b6d` is the first value at 3:1 on a uniform, hue-keeping darkening of the design's colour (3.03:1). `--color-control-border` keeps `#8c8374` (it borders the page, 3.38:1).
   - The light violating digit is `#8f1d17` (the design's `--violation-ink`), not `#b3261e`: the design's digit is 4.33:1 on the darkest band of the stripe, and an 8×8 digit on a 320 px phone is not large text, so 4.5 applies. The dark digit keeps the design's `#ff8a7d` (4.61:1).
   - A given cell in a violation keeps weight 800: the design's violation rule sets 700 over the given 800, which would remove the only cue of a given cell. The rule is `.cell-given.cell-violation { font-weight: 800 }`; it costs no pixel, because the design fixture's violating cells are all player cells.

   The pixel cost of the first two is small areas repeated in many shots and is NOT measured yet; the per-block tool measures it after the port. If a shot loses its 0.98 over them, the alternative is a design round that adopts the values (zero pixel cost) and the user moves the reference. Not decided here.
4. **The given cue is weight, not a border (ADR-worthy; PA-Q1 A).** A given cell is `aria-disabled` (FR-69), an inactive component, to which WCAG 1.4.11 asks no 3:1. The cue is weight 800 against 500, in a violation too; the fill, the darker ink and the missing inset line are decoration. The 2px given border and its pairs go; the violation widths become 1, 1, 2, 2. Trade-off: a weaker cue than a 3:1 border (accepted by the signature); `aria-disabled` is the non-visual counterpart, and the digit keeps 4.5:1. An ADR is proposed at the review gate (not written here).
5. **The solved state keys on the existing hook, not on `:has(`.** The design reads `.board[data-solved]` and `#app:has(.board[data-solved])`. FR-65 allows one `:has(` (the idle line). The page's host carries `data-solved="true"` (block 1), so the plate rule is `.board-host[data-solved='true'] .board`, the cells `.board-host[data-solved='true'] .cell`, and the button swap `.board-host[data-solved='true'] ~ .buttons …`, which block 1 already uses for weights. Trade-off: the selector differs from the design's; the computed paint is the same, and the e2e `win` case proves it.
6. **The underline is written with longhands.** The design writes `text-decoration: underline` (shorthand) for the violating digit and for «Скинути». The colour scan flags the shorthand because it cannot read its colour part. The port uses `text-decoration-line`, `text-decoration-thickness` and `text-underline-offset`; the computed paint is identical, and the scan needs no exception.
7. **Translucent colours live in tokens (ADR-worthy; PA-Q4).** The stripe and the hover ring are the opaque composites of the design's translucent colours over the only background they lie on (`--color-violation-bg`, a plain cell), so the pixels are the same and the tokens stay `#rrggbb`. The win glow is truly translucent: `--color-success-glow` is `#rrggbbaa` (`#2f6b3a73` light, `#8fd19a73` dark, alpha rounded at 8 bits), is excluded from every contrast pair, and replaces the design's `color-mix(in srgb, var(--success) 45%, transparent)`, which the scan forbids. The glow runs only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; the capture forces reduced motion, so no shot shows it. Trade-off: a second value format in the token reader; accepted, because later blocks need it for backdrops and shadows.
8. **Non-colour variables stay on `#app`.** `:root` holds only the colour tokens and `color-scheme`. `--hover-filter` (a `filter`, not a colour) would go on `#app`, its dark value in `:root[data-theme="dark"] #app`, which the scan treats as an ordinary rule. Block 2 does not port the hover filter: hover is not captured, and the filter also applies to the sheet and panel buttons of other blocks. The scenario guards the place if a later block ports it. (The brief said "if ported"; this is the resolution.)
9. **Text colours that were `--color-control-border` move to `--color-text-muted`.** The control border is now `#8c8374` (3.38:1 on the page), a 3:1 border colour; as a text colour it would fail 4.5:1 and axe would flag it (NFR-13). There are two uses: the idle line and `.mini-sep` in the rules panel (outside the main column; moved anyway, because no rule may use the border as text).
10. **A hinted cell gets its own binding.** The brief's binding list does not name `.cell-hinted`, but its new pairs (primary against the soft fill, the plate and the page) mean nothing unless a rule binds them. The spec binds `.cell-hinted` to `--color-primary`, `--color-primary-soft` and `--color-text`; its dashed border and italic digit are the non-colour cues.
11. **The paint check compares the page with the design build, frozen (ADR-worthy: a declared acceptance method needs a mechanism).** NFR-14's pixel gate is `npm run check:visual`; per-block work needs a finer, deterministic instrument. `scripts/freeze-design-paint.mjs` freezes computed paint (not pixels) into `quality/design-paint.json`, and the e2e test compares the page with it. Computed paint is exact and cheap, and it localises a mismatch to an element and a property. It is blind to what it does not read: the stripe's colour stops (the design's are translucent, the page's opaque; the stylesheet scenarios pin the page's), hover and active feedback, the glow, and any element or state outside the 10 cases. The pixel crops of `scripts/visual-block.mjs` are the independent second instrument. Both are `sampled`.

## Data model

- **Tokens.** 25 names; two value sets (`:root`, `:root[data-theme="dark"]`); `#rrggbb`, except `--color-success-glow` (`#rrggbbaa`). The token reader gains the translucent format; the contrast function still rejects it, so a translucent token can never enter a pair by mistake.
- **`quality/design-paint.json`.** `provenance` (design commit, build SHA-1, CSS SHA-1, reference SHA1SUMS, browser), `coverage: "sampled"`, `sampling` (schemes, states, viewport), `elements` (key to selector, with the cell kinds), `properties`, `deviations` (three deviation groups, each `{ elements: [...], properties: [...], scheme, cases, design, expected, reason }` with its A-57 clause: the light cell and given borders on every cell kind and side in all light cases; the light violating digit; the given-in-violation weight), `cases` (`{ shot, scheme, state, captureBoard, afterLoad?, paint: { <element>: { <property>: value } }, focus: { cell, button } }`).
- **States reached.** Default, four, eight and win at mount through the capture board (FR-119); hint by the capture board without its hinted cell and one press of `[data-action="hint"]`, as the geometry fixture does; dark by the browser context's colour scheme (no stored preference, so the theme is the system one in both builds).

## Error handling strategy

- The freeze script fails loudly: a missing build, an element not found in a case where the spec expects it, a non-deterministic re-run (two freezes of the same build must be byte-identical), or a path outside the build folder (the same path guard and malformed-escape answer as the geometry script). It never writes a partial fixture.
- The e2e test fails with one message per element, property and case; an element absent on one side is a miss, not a skip, except a zero-size element (an empty hint or win line paints nothing, as in block 1).
- A deviation is compared with its expected product value, never skipped; a difference outside the three deviation groups is a failure.
- Style errors surface as test failures with the rule and the token named. The page has no runtime error path in this change (CSS and three string copies only).

## What the browser checks cover, and what axe covers

- The stylesheet pair tests are the binding check for every contrast pair, on the literal token values. They hold in jsdom and need no layout.
- NFR-13's axe sweep (`npm run check:a11y`) checks text contrast in a real browser for the states it reaches: entries in the win state, the muted text, the primary text and the page text. It does NOT cover the violating digits (no axe state shows a violation), the given digits (axe skips `aria-disabled` elements), or any text over a `background-image` (axe reports "incomplete" for the stripe). For those, the pair tests are binding and the e2e palette test checks the computed colours.
- The e2e palette test covers computed paint at 10 cases; the pixel crops cover the rest of the pixels. Neither covers hover, active or the glow.

## Risks and mitigations

- **The light cell border is 3.03:1 against the plate, barely over 3:1.** Mitigation: the pair is a test on the literal value, so a change cannot slip.
- **The three deviations may cost pixels** across many shots (the board shows behind panels in the default, confirm, rules, settings and setup shots). Mitigation: measure with the per-block tool before any done claim; report the cost; the design-round alternative is documented.
- **Shared tokens move the unported controls.** The sheet, panels and dialog change colour through the tokens they bind (`--color-given-bg` as a border, `--color-focus` as a frame). Mitigation: NFR-13 and the theme tests run in the battery; the known failures (level card border, ink ring across a checked frame) are listed, not fixed here.
- **The dark focus ring changes** from `#f39a5b` (orange) to `#f1ede4` (ink). The checked option frame in the sheet and the panel uses `--color-focus`, so it was orange in dark and is now ink. Mitigation: it is the design's own finding 15; flagged for the sheet block.
- **A pair passes but the page looks wrong** (a token bound to the wrong rule). Mitigation: the cascade scenario reads the computed colours of every cell kind, and the e2e test compares them with the design's.
- **Capture determinism** (AGENTS.md lesson): the freeze reads computed styles after load with reduced motion and no animation running; a re-run must be byte-stable, otherwise it is a harness defect and not a finding.
