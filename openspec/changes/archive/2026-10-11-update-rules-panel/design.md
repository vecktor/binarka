# Design: update-rules-panel

## Context

G2 block A of the parallel plan (`docs/handoff/parallel-g2-plan-2026-10-11.md`), implemented by its own session in its own worktree, started from a `main` that already holds the merged `update-panel-shell`. The signed text is FR-57, FR-65, NFR-14, A-59, A-20 and A-26 in `docs/requirements.md` and `docs/handoff/rules-panel-amendment-draft-2026-10-11.md` (RQ-1 A, XQ-1 A, RQ-2 A with the 2rem cap, RQ-3 filled, RQ-4 no motion; autonomy-log row 172).

Today (`src/ui/style.css` ~631-733, before the shell): the panel is a 55dvh bottom sheet; from 48rem it is 28rem wide, centred, `height: fit-content`, with `max-height: 55dvh` still in force, so its contents scroll inside it; «Зрозуміло» is an ordinary full-width button; the panel opens at its end (the `autofocus` button is scrolled into view by the browser's focusing steps; `docs/qa/prep-g2/rules-open-probe.txt`). The design (`design/v0/app/binarka.css` lines 805-962, 1018-1047, 1122-1152) has the three regimes and a sticky, filled «Зрозуміло».

## Goals

- The panel has the design's size, place and paint at the three regimes, in light and in dark.
- The panel opens at its top on every opening, with focus on «Зрозуміло», by mouse and by keyboard.
- Every rules shot of the gate rises against run 9, and the box of the panel equals the design's within 0.5 px at the sampled viewports.
- The stylesheet stays inside the colour scan; no new token, no new pair.

## Non-goals

- Motion (RQ-4), the setup sheet, the settings panel, the dialog, the shell's tokens and bindings, a pixel floor, Safari and Firefox.
- No edit to `design/v0`, to the locked adapter, to `playwright.config.ts`, or to any baseline requirement other than «Rules panel».

## Decisions

1. **Sticky «Зрозуміло» plus a scroll reset on close and on open (RQ-1 A).** The probes decide it. With the design's `position: sticky; bottom: 0` alone the first opening is at the top (the button is already in view when the popover's focusing steps focus it, so nothing scrolls: `rules-open-probe.txt`, `scrollTop` 0 of 399, 292, 144 and 349 at 320×700, 375×812, 768×1024 and 1366×650 in the old geometry), but the second opening is at the end again, because Chromium restores a popover's scroll offset across hide and show (`rules-reopen-probe.txt`, 292 of 292 closed by the button or by Escape). The reset on **close** (`beforetoggle`, `newState` `closed`, while the panel is still shown) removes the stored offset before it can be restored, with no visible jump. The reset on **open** (`toggle`, `newState` `open`) is a net for a browser that scrolls the autofocused button into view anyway; Safari and Firefox are not tested here, and the user saw the bug in Chrome. Both are one assignment, `panel.scrollTop = 0`, per listener; the open reset must not undo a scroll made right after `:popover-open` (the locked `rules-techniques` driver scrolls to the end then), which the e2e checks with a frame of wait on each side of the scroll; opening and closing stay native (`popovertarget`), no popover method is called. Trade-off: two listeners and a small DOM-behaviour contract in `src/ui/play-page.ts`, against a panel that depends on an undocumented Chromium restoration. Alternatives, offered to the user and not taken: B (reset on open only: the first frame may show the old offset until `toggle`), C (drop `autofocus`, focus with `preventScroll`: changes the user's 2026-10-06 choice and the named-dialog scenario). Not ADR-worthy by itself (A-59 records it); an ADR is proposed only if a third panel copies the pattern.
2. **How a jsdom test sees the reset.** jsdom has no popover and no `ToggleEvent`. As in A-44 and A-46, the test dispatches a plain `Event` with `newState` and `oldState` added: the existing `dispatchToggle(el, 'open')` for `toggle`, and a new helper `dispatchBeforeToggle(el, 'closed')` in `tests/helpers/play-page.ts` for `beforetoggle` (which, unlike `dispatchToggle`, does not touch the stub's open state, because `beforetoggle` comes before the state change). The test sets `panel.scrollTop` to a number, dispatches, and reads 0. The same test proves the "no script" clause: the spied `showPopover`, `hidePopover` and `togglePopover` are never called by the reset. The premise that jsdom keeps an assigned `scrollTop` is asserted first; the fallback is an own accessor on the panel.
3. **The `min-height` through the root.** The design's formula `min(calc(100dvh - 7rem), calc(min(var(--board-width), var(--column)) + 13.875rem))` reads `--board-width`, which the design declares on `#app` and the page declared on `.board`. The shell (XQ-1 A) moved the five size variables to `#app` keyed on the root's `data-size`, so the panel, inside the root, copies the design's formula as it is. No `:has(` (FR-65 allows one). The constant is derived from the design's own comment (`binarka.css` 1012-1016): the summary button ends at 9.25rem; below it the 1rem gap, the board (B), the 1rem gap, the 2.75rem button row and, 0.5rem under it, the three reserved message lines end at 19.875rem + B; the panel starts at 6rem, so it needs 13.875rem + B. Trade-off: the panel depends on the shell's hook (an unmerged shell breaks it, so this folder starts after the shell's merge). The rejected alternatives are XQ-1 B (use `--column` for B: too tall for a 4×4 board and on short windows) and C (no `min-height`: the 768 shot would show the board under the panel's bottom edge). The geometry cases include a 4×4 board at 768×1024 and an 800×600 window so that an approximation cannot pass.
4. **`.rules:popover-open` for the flex column, never the bare `.rules`.** A `display` on `.rules` overrides the user agent's `[popover]:not(:popover-open) { display: none }` and shows the closed panel at load. A stylesheet scenario pins that no rule exactly `.rules` declares `display`. The spare height sits above «Зрозуміло» (`margin-top: auto`), never below it. `height: fit-content` is the user agent's popover value; the page states it, as it does today.
5. **The 2rem cap stays (RQ-2).** `max-width: calc(100% - 2rem)` is today's rule (`style.css` ~658), kept in the 48rem block. It changes no box: from 48rem the window is at least 48rem, so 100% less 2rem is at least 46rem, above 40rem and above the column plus 1rem (the column is `max(26rem, six cells of at most 5rem plus gaps)`, about 32rem at most). It is a departure from the design text, said plainly in the requirement.
6. **The example cells use `--color-control-border`.** The design's `--cell-border` has the values `#8c8374` (light) and `#8a7f71` (dark), which are the page's `--color-control-border` values. `--color-cell-border` is the light `#847b6d`, which A-57 darkened for the board's cells only (deviation 1); binding it here would add an unrecorded light deviation. The answer cell is a dashed `--color-primary` border on `--color-primary-soft` with `--color-text` (the hinted cell's look), and `--color-focus` no longer paints it.
7. **No motion (RQ-4).** The slide and fade (`binarka.css` 1508-1560) are under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; the gate captures with reduced motion, and the backdrop block left the backdrop fade out too. A stylesheet scenario pins that no `.rules` rule is in that media block or in `@starting-style`, so a later block that ports motion changes the scenario by its own delta.
8. **The design's inner metrics are part of the requirement.** From 64rem the panel is `height: fit-content`, so its box height is the content height: the heading size, the item font size and line height, the item gap, the techniques margin and `h3` all move the box. The page differs from the design in every one of these (heading 1.1rem against 1.125rem, item gap 6px against 0.5rem, line height 1.4 against 1.45, no `h3` or techniques rule, panel radius 12px against 1rem). Without them the geometry cases at 1024, 1366 and 1440 cannot pass. They are `.rules*` rules, inside the block's share of `style.css`. `.rules-close` also needs `padding: 1px 6px`: the page's base `button` rule has `padding: 10px 16px`, which makes the box 48 px tall, while the design has no base button rule, takes the user agent's padding and is 44 px tall (the `min-height` of 2.75rem wins). The value is the design's effective one, not a design declaration; confirmed on the design build (computed `padding` `1px 6px`, height 44 px, `docs/qa/update-rules-panel/sticky-ring-probe.txt`), and the geometry test decides. They go beyond the list in the amendment draft; see "Open points". The text and the DOM are the same on both sides (checked by reading `design/v0/components/binarka-page.tsx` 321-366 against `src/ui/strings.ts` `RULES` and `TECHNIQUES` and `RULE_EXAMPLES` in `src/ui/play-page.ts`: the three rules, the three techniques, the example tokens `0 0 1?`, `0 1 0 1?` and `0 1 1 0 ≠ 1 0 0 1`, the `rule-text` and `rule-example` spans, the `techniques` section with its `h3`), so the line breaks, and with them the content height from 64rem, depend on CSS alone.
9. **The geometry cases go under their own key.** `e2e/nfr-14-layout-geometry.spec.ts:49` pins `fixture.cases` to the 32 expected shots, and the layout block of `scripts/visual-block.mjs` mirrors those 16 selectors, so appending rules cases to `cases` would fail an unrelated test. The key `rulesPanel` (`selectors`, `coverage`, `cases`) is new; the freeze script writes it and leaves the rest as it was. The script derives its cases from `quality/visual-parity.config.json`; the rules cases need a press (the panel is not open at mount on the page), a 4×4 board with the panel open (the design has no such route) and an 800×600 viewport that is in no breakpoint of the config. The script therefore gains an explicit list of the 8 cases and an explicit `captureBoard` per case (the two synthetic names parse to states for which `captureBoardFor` returns null) and the recorded after-load action `open-rules`, like the existing `press-hint`, which the page side replays. On the design side the `/rules/` route is already open at load (a press would close it), so the six review-set cases and the 800×600 case load `/rules/` and wait for `:popover-open`; only the 4×4 case loads `/four/` and presses «Правила». The six review-set viewports use the config's own shot names (`320-light-rules` and so on); the two extra cases are `768-light-rules-four` and `800-light-rules-short`. The script rewrites the whole `provenance` block on every run (lines 107-126), and `designBuildSha1` differs between worktrees (`cdf4751f…` in a fresh one against the recorded `feb08415…`, with the same `designCommit`, `designCssSha1` and browser), so a plain refreeze would change the existing provenance. The edit keeps the recorded top-level `provenance` when `designCommit` and `designCssSha1` are unchanged and writes the run's build hash and browser into `rulesPanel.provenance`. Neither the script nor `visual-block.mjs` is in `factory-lock.json` (checked by grep), so both are ordinary edits.
10. **Source order (re-audit findings 2 and 3).** The 22.5rem `.rules` and `.mini` rules and the 48rem `.rules:popover-open`, `.rules > *`, techniques and `.rules-close` rules must come later in the file than the top-level `.rules`, `.mini`, `.rules-close` and techniques rules, or the top-level `padding`, `width` and `margin` win at the same specificity (as in the design, where the overrides follow the base rules). The existing 22.5rem block at line 451 and the 48rem block at 671 are too early for them; the port places them in a later block (for example after the panel's top-level rules), and the first exact `.rules` rule of the raw text stays the top-level one, which the shell's raw counts read. The one exact `.rules` rule of the 48rem block (line 677) stays where it is: it only overrides the top-level `.rules`, which is earlier.
11. **One `.rules` rule per at-rule.** The shell's stylesheet tests read "the rule" `.rules` inside `@media (min-width: 48rem)` (for its `box-shadow` and `border-color`). The design writes the 48rem `min-height` as a second `.rules` rule; here its declarations are merged into the one rule, so the shell's tests stay green without an edit. The same holds for the 64rem block. If a shell test still fails, it is a finding to put to the user, not a test to edit.

## Error handling strategy

- The page has no new runtime error path: the two listeners assign `scrollTop = 0` on an element that exists; a browser without `beforetoggle` or `toggle` keeps the other listener and the sticky button, and the panel still opens (only the second-opening reset can be missing there).
- The stylesheet cannot fail at runtime; a wrong token or a stray old binding is a stylesheet test failure that names the rule and the declaration.
- The e2e fails (never skips) with the case, the element and both boxes on any difference; it asserts the panel is `:popover-open` before it reads. The freeze script and the `rules` block fail with the build instruction of `design/README.md` when `design/v0/out/index.html` is missing.
- A capture-unstable run is a harness defect to file, not a pixel finding (AGENTS.md, capture determinism). Captures take the lock of task 0.2.

## Pixel expectations

- **All 20 rules shots fail in run 9** (`docs/qa/g2/check-visual-run-9.txt`): light 0.7767 (1024) to 0.9010 (375), dark 0.8168 (1024) to 0.9029 (375). The causes are the panel's size and place (all widths from 48rem), the scroll position (the page shows the end, the design the top), the outlined «Зрозуміло» and the paint.
- **The dark shots from 64rem** (1024, 1366, 1440) fell in run 8 because of masking (`docs/qa/visual-diff/README.md`, § Correction of Run 8): where the design shows its panel fill `#2c2620` and the page shows the bare page under the strong dim `rgb(15,12,9)`, the pixels now differ. The panel mismatch was always there; the placement and paint of this block remove it.
- **`768-dark-rules`** fell from 0.9314 (run 7) to 0.8882 (run 8, soft dim). By the gate's own per-pixel verdict (pixelmatch 8.0.0; `docs/qa/prep-g2/soft-dim-768-classify.txt`), 135,776 of the 135,961 newly failing pixels are one pair: the design's panel fill `#2c2620` where the page shows its soft-dimmed bare page (`#120f0c` now, `#1a1714` before). It is the same masking under the soft dim and belongs to this block's placement.
- **Expected, not measured: every one of the 20 rules shots rises against run 9.** No 0.98 claim per shot: text rendering inside the panel, the board under the backdrop and the shell paint all contribute. The per-block tool (`node scripts/visual-block.mjs --block rules --states rules,rules-techniques`) localises any residual. **If a rules shot does not rise, the block stops and explains it before the review gate.** A fall is reported as measured.

## Risks and mitigations

- **The stuck button's focus ring may be clipped.** At the panel's bottom edge the 3px ring at a 2px offset extends 5px below a stuck button, outside the scroll container's padding box, and an `overflow: auto` container clips what lies outside its padding box. The design shows the same geometry; the left, right and top sides stay inside the 1rem padding. Mitigation: the new e2e measures it at 320×700 and 375×812 while the button is stuck (first opening and after scrolling to the end): the close button's rect plus 5px must lie inside the panel's rect less its borders, on the bottom, left and right; the smoke test (task 6.1) looks at it by eye. (The `nfr-13` keyboard sweep checks that an outline exists, not that it is unclipped, so it is not the mitigation.) If the check fails, the block stops and reports it; a fix would be a departure from the design and needs the user. **Measured on the design build after the shell's merge** (`docs/qa/update-rules-panel/sticky-ring-probe.txt`): in Chromium the stuck button respects the scroll container's bottom padding, so it sits 16 px above the panel's inner bottom edge at 320×700, 375×812, 768×1024 and 1366×650, on the first opening and at the end (16.25 at 320 end), and 16 px from the left and right; the 5 px ring fits. The risk stays for the page until the e2e measures it there.
- **The panel's content height differs from the design's** (type metrics). Mitigation: decision 8; the geometry cases at 1024, 1366 and 1440 compare the box; `scripts/visual-block.mjs --block rules` localises a residual.
- **The sticky and the reset hide a defect only a second opening shows.** Mitigation: the e2e makes a second opening at every viewport and asserts which viewports scroll.
- **Shell tests fail on a second `.rules` rule** (decisions 10 and 11). Mitigation: merge declarations; report, never edit the shell's tests.
- **Dark bands and the stale 768 soft-dim drop.** Expected and explained above.
- **Safari and Firefox are untested** (NFR-7).
- **A shot falls** (see "Pixel expectations"): the block stops and explains it.

## Open points

1. **Beyond the amendment's list.** The inner type metrics and the radii (panel 1rem, «Зрозуміло» 0.625rem, example cells 0.375rem) are the design's values; A-59 says "the values are the design's", but the draft's section 2 lists only the regimes, the spacing, the sticky button and the paint. They are in the ADDED requirement because the geometry test cannot pass without the metrics. The user may strike the radii (a pixel cost only).
2. **FR-57 says the page "resets" the scroll position when the panel closes and opens**; the amendment draft's section 2 said "may reset". The folded `docs/requirements.md` text is the signed one and is used.
3. **The second-opening check cannot scroll at 768×1024 and 1366×650**, where the panel fits (the design's claim for 1366×650). The e2e asserts that, and still makes the second opening there. The reset is therefore exercised where the panel scrolls: 320×700 and 375×812.
