# Design: update-page-backdrop

## Context

G2 block 3 (autonomy-log row 148; blocks 1 and 2 archived as `2026-10-10-update-page-layout-geometry` and `2026-10-10-update-page-palette`). Block 2 ported the main column's paint and declared 25 of the 34 tokens of the appendix to A-57. The two backdrop tokens are signed (A-57 appendix, "later"; FR-65: a translucent token "is never the foreground or the background of a contrast pair, and a backdrop is checked composited over the page"). This block declares them and binds them to the four surfaces. No requirement text changes, so no signature is needed; the specs change only where they said "25", "one translucent token" or "the backdrops keep their current bindings".

Today's page paints a backdrop with `background-color: var(--color-text)` and an `opacity` (confirm 0.45, the other three 0.3) and, in dark, overrides it with `--color-page` at 0.6 and 0.7 (`src/ui/style.css`: `.confirm::backdrop` ~571, `.rules::backdrop` ~614, `.setup-sheet::backdrop` ~768, `.settings::backdrop` ~1051, the dark rules ~1055-1065). In dark that dim is a no-op: the page colour at any opacity over the page is the page colour, `rgb(26, 23, 20)`. The design dims with a translucent brown (light) or near-black (dark) and does not use `opacity`.

## Goals

- Each surface's backdrop is the design's colour, from a token, in light and in dark.
- A backdrop dims the page in both schemes, checked as the composite over `--color-page` (FR-65), not as a contrast pair.
- The stylesheet stays inside the colour scan (tokens only, no `color-mix(`, no `!important`, one `:has(`), and no `::backdrop` rule sets `opacity`.

## Non-goals

- The panels', the sheet's and the dialog's own paint and shadows (later blocks); the backdrop fade and any motion (decision 3); `design/v0` is not edited.
- A pixel floor for any shot.

## Decisions

1. **Token values and the surface map are the design's, value for value.** `--color-backdrop-soft` is `rgba(70, 40, 15, 0.22)` in the design and `#46280f38` here (alpha 0x38 = 56/255 = 0.2196); strong is `rgba(45, 25, 10, 0.5)`, `#2d190a80` (0x80 = 128/255 = 0.5020); dark soft `rgba(8, 5, 2, 0.45)`, `#08050273`; dark strong `rgba(8, 5, 2, 0.6)`, `#08050299`. The appendix rounds at 8 bits, so the page's alpha differs from the design's by up to 0.002; the browser check allows 1 per channel. The map: rules soft, and strong from 64rem (`binarka.css` ~1083, `.rules:popover-open::backdrop`); the setup sheet soft (the design's `.setup`, the page's `.setup-sheet`); settings soft at every width ("a small dropdown", the design's comment ~1080); confirm strong (~1311). The appendix's role column names one surface per token ("settings backdrop", "rules and dialog backdrop"); the spec's role column is fuller and the values are untouched. Trade-off: the spec's roles are more exact than the appendix's; accepted, the appendix is not edited by this change (open question 2).
2. **A backdrop is checked as a composite, not as a pair (FR-65).** The composite is `round(alpha × token + (1 − alpha) × page)` per channel in sRGB (the values the browser blends for a plain `background-color`, not linear light), then the relative luminance of the result. The requirement is "lower than the page's", not a ratio floor: the dark composites are 1.07:1 and 1.09:1 against the page, so any ratio threshold would fail the design's own dark dim, which is a legitimate dim of a near-black page. The four composites are pinned in the scenarios (light soft `rgb(208, 198, 186)`, strong `rgb(146, 134, 122)`; dark soft `rgb(18, 15, 12)`, strong `rgb(15, 12, 9)`), computed over the page only. `contrastRatio` and `rgbOf` keep refusing `#rrggbbaa`, so a translucent value cannot enter a pair by mistake; the composite is a new helper. This is the "how a translucent token is verified" half of the ADR-worthy decision 7 of `update-page-palette` (translucent colours live in tokens, PA-Q4): if the user asks for that ADR, it should cover the composite check too. The old check ("no lighter than the page", by `contrastRatio` with white) is replaced because it cannot read a translucent value; the light counterpart is new because the design's light backdrop dims too.
3. **The backdrop fade is not ported.** The design's transitions and `@starting-style` for the backdrops (`binarka.css` 1585-1610) sit inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` (opens at 1468). Three facts decide. (a) The page animates neither the sheet, the panels nor the dialog (its only motion is the win glow); a backdrop that fades while the surface it belongs to appears at once looks wrong. (b) The gate's capture forces reduced motion (`scripts/check-visual-parity-adapters.mjs`: `reducedMotion: 'reduce'`, `animations: 'disabled'`, 300 ms settle), so neither the frozen reference nor the page capture shows the fade in any shot; it has zero pixel effect on NFR-14. (c) NFR-14 is this block's driver. Trade-off: a user without the reduced-motion preference sees the backdrop appear at once, where the design fades it in over 220 ms; accepted, and recorded as a gap, not a defect. The fade belongs with the sheet and dialog motion in a later block, and a scenario pins its absence so that adding it is a deliberate spec change. Not ADR-worthy by itself; it becomes one if the user decides motion is never ported. Open question 1.
4. **Longhand `background-color`, no `opacity`.** The design writes `background: var(--backdrop-soft)`. The page writes `background-color`: the colour scan allows the `background` shorthand only with a token colour, but its longhand clause ("every `background-color` value is a single `var(--color-...)`") is stronger and simpler to test. The cascade is the same. `opacity` on a `::backdrop` fades the whole backdrop, colour and alpha together; with the alpha in the token it is redundant, and it was the reason the old dark dim did nothing.
5. **The strong backdrop for the rules panel wins by specificity.** `.rules:popover-open::backdrop` (one pseudo-class more than `.rules::backdrop`) inside `@media (min-width: 64rem)`, as the design has it. Source order is then irrelevant, so the rule can sit in the existing `@media (min-width: 64rem)` block (~1025) or next to the base rule. A `::backdrop` exists only while the surface is open, so `:popover-open` adds nothing but specificity.
6. **No dark-only override.** The dark values come from the token set; the two dark override rules and their comment are deleted. The scenario forbids a selector that starts with `:root[data-theme` and contains `::backdrop`.
7. **A sampled pixel check in a real browser, besides the stylesheet test (declared-method-needs-mechanism).** NFR-14's pixel gate is `npm run check:visual`, which decides per shot and is too coarse for a block; a stylesheet test reads declarations, not paint. `e2e/nfr-14-backdrop.spec.ts` opens each surface in the built page and reads one screenshot pixel that no surface covers, after reading the same pixel with the surface closed (it must be the page colour, so a covered pixel fails instead of passing by luck). It does not rely on `getComputedStyle(el, '::backdrop')`, whose support for popovers and dialogs is not verified here; the test-engineer may try it on a spike and use it as a secondary assertion only. Coverage is sampled: surfaces (rules at 2 widths, setup, settings, confirm), schemes (2), 10 cases. The screenshot was to be decoded with `pngjs`, which is already a dependency (imported by `scripts/visual-block.mjs`). **Deviation found in the red phase (2026-10-11):** `pngjs` ships no types, `tsc --noEmit` covers `e2e/`, and `@types/pngjs` would be a new dependency. So the spec decodes the screenshot with a small 8-bit RGB/RGBA PNG reader on `node:zlib` inside the file. No dependency is added. The reader is checked twice: the closed-pixel reads equal the page colour in 10 of 10 cases, and the secondary `getComputedStyle(el, '::backdrop')` read agrees with the pixel within 1 per channel (`docs/qa/update-page-backdrop/red-run.txt`, sections D and the spike). The sample pixel is chosen from the open surface's bounding rect (a point outside it, inside the viewport); a case with no uncovered pixel is a failure to fix, never a skip. It does not cover widths between the two, hover, or the no-preference motion.

## Data model

- **Tokens (27).** The 25 of block 2 plus `--color-backdrop-soft` and `--color-backdrop-strong`; two value sets; `#rrggbb`, except three `#rrggbbaa` (the glow and the two backdrops). `TRANSLUCENT_TOKENS` holds the three; no contrast pair names one.
- **Composites over the page** (the expected pixels):

| Scheme | Page | Soft (alpha 0.2196 light, 0.4510 dark) | Strong (alpha 0.5020 light, 0.6000 dark) |
|---|---|---|---|
| light | `#f7f3ea` (L 0.8982) | `rgb(208, 198, 186)` (0.5734) | `rgb(146, 134, 122)` (0.2457) |
| dark | `#1a1714` (L 0.0088) | `rgb(18, 15, 12)` (0.0050) | `rgb(15, 12, 9)` (0.0038) |

- **Today's composites over the page** (for the estimate below): light 0.3 of `#1e2126` is `rgb(182, 180, 175)`; light confirm at 0.45 is `rgb(149, 149, 146)`; dark at any opacity is `rgb(26, 23, 20)`.

## Error handling strategy

- The page has no runtime error path in this change (CSS only). A wrong token or a stray `opacity` surfaces as a stylesheet test failure that names the rule and the token.
- The composite helper throws on anything that is not `#rrggbbaa` over an opaque `#rrggbb` page, so a mistaken call is a loud failure, not a silent pass; its self-tests pin the four composites.
- The e2e test fails with one message per surface, scheme and width. A closed pixel that is not the page colour is a failure of the case (the sample point was covered or the surface did not close), never a skip.
- Capture determinism (AGENTS.md lesson): the e2e test uses reduced motion, the scheme on the context and no stored preference. Before any capture of this block, check `lsof -nP -iTCP:4173 -sTCP:LISTEN` (the e2e preview, `playwright.config.ts`) and the same for 4174, 4176 and 4177 for an orphaned `vite preview` of this worktree (run 7 had one: README, run 7 section). A re-run of the pixel test must give the same pixel; an unstable capture is a harness defect to file, not a finding.

## What should move (expected, not measured)

The estimate uses the checker's own per-pixel rule (pixelmatch threshold 0.1: a pixel differs when its YIQ delta exceeds 35215 × 0.1² = 352) on the backdrop over the bare page; over lighter content the numbers are of the same order. Old against new: light soft (rules below 64rem, setup, settings) delta 209, under 352; light rules from 64rem (old `rgb(182, 180, 175)`, new strong `rgb(146, 134, 122)`) delta 990, over; light confirm (old `rgb(149, 149, 146)`, new strong) delta 108, under; dark soft delta 32 and dark strong delta 61, under. So:

- **Expected to move a great deal:** the five light rules shots from 64rem (1024 `rules`; 1366 and 1440, each for `rules` and `rules-techniques`; there is no 1024 `rules-techniques` shot; "six" corrected in review fix round 1), 0.13 to 0.22 in run 7. Almost every pixel there is a backdrop pixel, and the old one was over the tolerance. They are expected to land near their neighbours (the same shots at 320 to 768 in light, 0.89 to 0.90, and the dark desktop shots, 0.93 to 0.955), and not above 0.98: the panel's own fill, border and shadow are unported.
- **Expected to move little:** the other light rules, settings and setup shots (old and new soft are inside the per-pixel tolerance), the light confirm shots (inside it), and every dark shot (the dark dims differ by 8 to 11 per channel). Their residual is the surfaces' own paint, which later blocks own. The README's remark that the missing backdrop dominates the light `confirm` and `rules` shots holds for the six desktop rules shots; for confirm this estimate says otherwise, so do not expect confirm to pass on this block alone.
- **Remains for later blocks:** the panel fill, border, radius and shadow (`--color-surface-raised`, `--color-overlay-border`, `--color-shadow-panel`), the dialog's paint, and the setup sheet (run 7: 0.75 to 0.94; `--color-option-border`, `--color-violation-ink`, the sunken and sheet shadows, «Почати»). Per-shot results of `npm run check:visual` are measured after the port and reported as they are.

The per-block tool `scripts/visual-block.mjs --block layout --states rules,rules-techniques,confirm,setup,settings` reads the pixel channel over the union of the main column's boxes, which is where the dimmed page shows; it does not cover the page margins outside the column boxes. The whole viewport is read by run 8 of the gate (open question 3).

## Risks and mitigations

- **The estimate is wrong.** It is a back-of-envelope on the bare page. Mitigation: run 8 and the per-block crops decide, and any claim in `current-state.md` is the measured one with an evidence path.
- **The strong rule loses the cascade** and the rules panel keeps the soft backdrop from 64rem. Mitigation: the scenario pins the selector inside the 64rem block; the e2e test reads the strong composite at 1024×768 and the soft one at 375×812.
- **A new `::backdrop` rule appears elsewhere** (a later surface) with its own colour. Mitigation: the "only the five selectors" clause fails first; the author adds a row by delta.
- **Shared tokens.** The two new tokens bind only the `::backdrop` rules, so no other element changes colour. `--color-text` and `--color-page` stay bound elsewhere when the old backdrops go.
- **`:popover-open::backdrop` support.** The page already needs `popover` and `:popover-open` for the three popover surfaces (A-44), so the selector adds no new support requirement. In a browser that drops the selector the rules panel keeps the soft backdrop from 64rem, and nothing else depends on the rule.
- **A surface that stays open between e2e cases.** A `::backdrop` exists only while its surface is open; the closed-pixel read guards against a case that starts with a surface still open.

## Open questions

1. **The backdrop fade** (`transition`, `@starting-style`; decision 3). Default: not ported in this block; revisit with the sheet and dialog motion in a later block. Taking it now would add rules that no shot can see (the capture forces reduced motion).
2. **The appendix's role text** for the two backdrop rows ("settings backdrop", "rules and dialog backdrop") is narrower than the spec's role column (decision 1). Default: leave `docs/requirements.md` alone; the values are identical and the roles are descriptive, so no signature is needed.
3. **The per-block tool's region** (`scripts/visual-block.mjs --block layout` reads only the main column's boxes, so the dimmed page margins are read only by run 8 of the gate). Default: rely on run 8; do not extend the tool in this slice.
