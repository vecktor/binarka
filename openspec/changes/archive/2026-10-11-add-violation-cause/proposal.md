# Change: add-violation-cause

## Why

Today every cell of a broken group looks the same. `refreshHighlights` (`src/ui/play-page.ts:249-269`) gives every cell of every violation the class `cell-violation` and `aria-invalid="true"`; for the count rule it marks the whole line (6 or 8 cells). Nothing says which press made the violation. The user asked for a mark on that cell (autonomy-log row 159, in chat on 2026-10-11 at about 00:20) and signed the amendment in chat at about 09:44 (UTC+5:30): CQ-1 «A» (the name suffix) and the defaults for CQ-2 to CQ-7 (autonomy-log rows 162 and 163). The signed text is in `docs/requirements.md`: **FR-120** (new), **A-58** (new), and FR-64, FR-65 and FR-70 amended; the reasoning, the scenario list P1 to P8 and the auditor's findings are in `docs/handoff/violation-cause-amendment-draft-2026-10-11.md`. The design touch is done, reviewed (`docs/design/cause-touch-2026-10-11/REVIEW.md`) and committed (`c87c679`, with its stills); the user adopted its forced-colours rule and kept the mark's size as signed.

This is a change folder, not a block of G2 (the NFR-14 reference does not move: no shot of the 170 shows the mark, `docs/qa/violation-cause-amendment/hint-shot-check.txt`).

## What Changes

- **`play-page` spec, ADDED (1):** «Cause marker» (FR-120): the rule for the class `cell-cause` (set exactly when the checker's cells include the pressed cell), its life (the removing table, the non-removing table, the confirmation timing, none at mount, two mounts independent, at most one cell, always also `cell-violation`, never a given, never empty), and scenarios P1 to P8, identical lines, the hinted cause, a failed generation, the language switch and an oracle sweep at N = 4, 6 and 8.
- **`play-page` spec, MODIFIED (7):**
  - «A violation shows a cue besides colour» (FR-64): the sentence "The cue is applied through the existing class `cell-violation` (no new class), …" becomes "The violation cue is applied through the class `cell-violation`, …"; a new paragraph describes the cause mark (a `::after` triangle drawn with the six border longhands, legs about a quarter of the inner width with a `0.5rem` floor, `position: relative` on `.cell-cause` only, the forced-colours block); a new paragraph "Intentionally not delivered" lists the 3px border, a rounded mark, motion, a mark on a non-cause cell, a mark at mount and a larger mark at 8×8 on 320px; `Traces:` gains FR-120, FR-65 and A-58; four scenarios are added (the stylesheet text, the cell's own border, the forced-colours block, a sampled browser read of the mark's corner pixel).
  - «Cell labels» (FR-70): the cause cell appends «, спричиняє порушення» after «, підказка»; a violation gets no suffix "unless it is the cause cell"; the suffix follows the marker (the cell that loses it is relabelled at once); a new sentence says a given is never the cause, so its label is «, задано» only; a new sentence records the English wording ", causes a violation" as confirmed by the user on 2026-10-11 (autonomy-log row 164); `Traces:` gains FR-120; the scenario «A violation adds no suffix» is replaced by «Only the cause cell of a violation gets a suffix» (with an AND on the board of P2); four scenarios are added.
  - «Cells expose a Ukrainian name and their state» (FR-70, FR-61): the name lists the third suffix in both languages; the label is rewritten when the cell changes "or gains or loses the marker `cell-hinted` or `cell-cause`"; a new sentence: the cause cell has no other ARIA attribute, no `aria-describedby` and no element; the AND of «Names of a fresh board» adds `cell-cause` to the label's inputs; «A violation is exposed to assistive technology» gains an AND (the cause suffix on the clicked cell, none on the other two, no `aria-describedby`); `Traces:` gains FR-120.
  - «Borders, cues and focus rings have enough contrast» (FR-65): the first sentence gains the one narrow exception to "every colour is a token" (the system colour keyword `ButtonText` in the forced-colours block); the colour scan lists the 42 CSS Color 4 system colours (19 current, 23 deprecated) and allows exactly one declaration, pinned by a new scan; `.cell-cause::after` (the top-level rule) joins the list of rules that take their colours from tokens; the cause cue's pair against the stripe (4.33 / 4.61) beside the fill (5.38 / 6.24) joins the 3:1 list; the token AND of «Rules use the tokens and no colour literal is left» is extended with the top-level `.cell-cause::after`; three scenarios are added (the cause cue's pairs, the accepted adjacencies with the hover ring at 2.45 / 2.00, the system-colour scan); `Traces:` gains FR-120 and A-58.
  - Three edits **beyond the draft's §5 list**, each one token or one row, justified in `design.md` decision 9: «English page text» (the table row; `cellLabel` takes a sixth argument; a parity assertion; `Traces:` gains FR-70 and FR-120), «Page text is per mode» and «Ukrainian page text» (the cell-name regex of one scenario each gains the suffix).
- **`src/ui/play-page.ts`, `src/ui/strings.ts`, `src/ui/style.css`:** the marker next to `refreshHighlights`, with the relabel of the cell that loses it; the suffix in both languages (`cellLabel` gains `cause`); the `.cell-cause` rules and the forced-colours block.
- **`docs/requirements.md` FR-65, outside this folder (tasks 1.1, done):** one clause naming the system-colour exception, added by the parent with the user's yes (autonomy-log row 164) in commit `0348f0d`, before the folder audit.
- **`design/v0/app/binarka.css`:** the forced-colours block is mirrored, and the one stale comment clause that says forced colours show the mark "as a filled square" (775-777) is corrected (the design touch, committed in `c87c679`, already holds `.cell-cause`); then `quality/design-paint.json` and `quality/design-geometry.json` are refrozen (provenance only; both are already stale today, at `designCommit` `2aa39ac…`).
- **New checks:** `tests/play-page-violation-cause.test.ts`, a stylesheet test of the mark, a capture-board case, and `e2e/nfr-14-cause-mark.spec.ts` (2 sampled cases: light 6×6 at 1024×768, dark 8×8 at 320×640). `playwright.config.ts` is not edited (the `layout` project's `testMatch` already has `nfr-14-*`).
